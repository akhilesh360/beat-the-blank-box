# Interview guide (plan — not yet run)

**Goal:** learn why new users stop after their first AI chat session, and which work tasks would make a good first win per role.
**Who:** 8–10 people who signed up for an AI chat tool in the last 30 days, mixed roles, including some who stopped using it.
**Length:** 30 minutes, recorded with consent.

## Warm-up (5 min)
1. What do you do day to day at work?
2. Which tools do you use most for that?

## First session (10 min)
3. Walk me through the first time you opened the app. What did you type?
4. What were you hoping would happen?
5. What happened instead? How did you feel about it?
6. Did you go back? Why or why not?

## Real tasks (10 min)
7. Think about last week. What tasks took longer than they should have?
8. Which of those, if any, have you tried with an AI tool?
9. (Show 3 starter tasks for their role.) Which, if any, would you try? Why that one?

## Wrap-up (5 min)
10. If you could change one thing about getting started, what would it be?
11. Anything I should have asked?

## Notes for the interviewer
- Ask about past behaviour, not hypotheticals. Don't pitch the idea before question 9.
- Record: role, first prompt typed, whether they returned, tasks named in Q7.
- Synthesis: group Q7 answers by role to pick the 3 starter tasks per role.
